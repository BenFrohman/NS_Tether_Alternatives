# Terminology note

## Classical structure
The classical Lie–Poisson bracket is antisymmetric and satisfies the Jacobi identity. That makes it a Poisson bracket. The coadjoint orbits of SDiff are its symplectic leaves.

## Tethered sum
The tethered bracket is the classical Lie–Poisson bracket plus a symmetric negative-semidefinite (or, in the non-symmetric experimental track, a non-symmetric) correction. The sum is metriplectic. It does not satisfy the Jacobi identity in general and does not have the same symplectic leaves.

Revised drafts use this language.
