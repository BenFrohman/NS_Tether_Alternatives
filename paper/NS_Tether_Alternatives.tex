\begin{abstract}
An exploratory repository of alternative tether designs. This note lists exactly what is machine-checked. No regularity result for Navier--Stokes is claimed.
\end{abstract}
\section{Machine-checked (theorem)}
Vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$ are formalized as maps $\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$.
The kernel is
\[
\texttt{NonSymmetricTetherKernel}\,\kappa\,\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\,e\,F\,G
=-\kappa\cdot\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\cdot\bigl((F\times e)\cdot G\bigr).
\]
Here $e:\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$ is a parameter of both \texttt{NonSymmetricTetherKernel} and \texttt{T\_on\_H}, exactly as in Lean.
The projection is $\Pi_u v=v-\bigl(\langle v,u\rangle/\langle u,u\rangle\bigr)u$, with $\Pi_0 v=v$.
\texttt{\#print axioms} on each theorem below is \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}. There is no \texttt{sorry}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{NonSymmetricTether.nonSymmetricTetherKernel\_diag}:
for all $\kappa,\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\in\mathbb{R}$ and all $e,F:\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$,
\[
\texttt{NonSymmetricTetherKernel}\,\kappa\,\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\,e\,F\,F=0.
\]
\item \texttt{NonSymmetricC2.Pi\_u\_self}:
for all $u:\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$,
\[
\Pi_u u=0.
\]
\item \texttt{NonSymmetricC2.T\_on\_H}:
for all $\kappa,\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\in\mathbb{R}$ and all $e,u,w:\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$,
\[
\texttt{NonSymmetricTetherKernel}\,\kappa\,\omega_{\mathrm{norm\_sq}}\,e\,(\Pi_u u)\,w=0.
\]
The Lean statement has no further hypothesis. The module docstring reads this as C2 in the first slot under the identification $\delta H=u$ (so that $\Pi_u\delta H=\Pi_u u$); that identification is not a hypothesis of the theorem. The result follows from $\Pi_u u=0$ and gives no damping or regularity bound.
\end{itemize}

\section{Stated, not machine-checked}
\begin{itemize}
\item The a-posteriori route and the $+\kappa$ option (prose only).
\item Any quantitative bound for the non-symmetric kernel. A canonical choice of $e$ at points where $\omega=0$ is not machine-checked: $e:\mathrm{Fin}\,3\to\mathbb{R}$ is a parameter of \texttt{NonSymmetricTetherKernel} and of \texttt{T\_on\_H}, and Lean does not set $e=0$.
\end{itemize}
\section{Structural obstruction}
An antisymmetric kernel dissipates nothing ($T(F,F)=0$). Together with C2 ($T(H,\cdot)=0$), antisymmetry forces $T(M,H)=0$, so this candidate cannot supply damping of $M$.
\section{Open item: \texorpdfstring{$\delta H=\psi$, not $u$}{delta H = psi, not u}}
In the vorticity pairing, $\frac{d}{dt}H(\omega+t\varepsilon)|_0=\int\langle u,\mathrm{BS}\,\varepsilon\rangle=\int\langle\psi,\varepsilon\rangle$. The hypothesis $\delta H=u$ is false as stated. Counterexample: $u=(\sin z,0,0)$, $\omega=(0,\cos z,0)$, $\varepsilon=\omega$. The derivative is $\int|u|^2>0$ while $\int\langle u,\omega\rangle=0$. The C2 reading of \texttt{T\_on\_H} that assumes $\delta H=u$ is conditional on that identification, which is not a hypothesis of the Lean theorem.
