# Geometric Observation

**Author:** Benjamin Stanley Frohman

## The observation

C2 forces
\[
\mathfrak{T}(H,\,\cdot\,)=0,
\]
so the tethered flow still conserves kinetic energy and the ideal Euler equations remain exactly the classical ones. At the same time the symmetric term can be nonzero on other functionals, so the bracket is non-degenerate away from the energy direction.

This cleanly separates the conservative skeleton from the dissipative directions.

## What it does not do

It does not by itself produce the damping estimate. Under the present hypotheses the C2/C3 obstruction remains: symmetry + C2 still force the pairing against any Lyapunov functional M to vanish, so the tether contributes nothing to \(\dot M\).

## Status

Geometric certificates remain kernel-closed. Analytic bounds remain named open. No regularity claim.
